## P1 - Uu tien cao

### Tai cau truc menu

Menu hien tai gom: Tra cuu, Theo benh, Hieu dung, Kien thuc, Thuc don, Cong cu, Tac gia, them tim kiem va theme toggle.

Van de:

- `Hieu dung` da co link `Tat ca bai viet`, sau do lai co tab `Kien thuc` cung dich den.
- `Thuc don` tro thang den cong cu ke hoach bua an, khong phai landing page gom thuc don.
- Dropdown `Theo benh` tron disease hubs, ban do benh va nhom doi tuong.
- Desktop qua nhieu diem cap mot; mobile tao menu rat dai va tang so thao tac.

Menu dich de xuat:

1. `Tra cuu`: Thuc pham, Mon an Viet, So sanh, Bang xep hang.
2. `Theo benh`: Tat ca nhom benh, 7 hub uu tien, Ban do benh.
3. `Bai viet`: Kien thuc nen tang, Dinh duong benh ly, Doi tuong, Dung tin ngay.
4. `Thuc don`: Trang tong hop thuc don, Lap thuc don, Danh sach di cho.
5. `Cong cu`: landing page cong cu va cac cong cu noi bat.
6. Tim kiem, Tac gia/Gioi thieu va theme toggle la utility navigation.

### Hop nhat taxonomy

- `src/data/disease-hubs.ts`: 7 hub duoc chuan hoa.
- `src/pages/ban-do-benh.astro`: 58 nhom khai bao truc tiep, phan lon khong co thuc don.
- `src/data/articles.ts`: 310 metadata bai; `specialty` du 310, `audience` chi co 168.

De xuat mot schema duy nhat cho `specialty`, `condition`, `audience`, `contentType`, `journeyStage`, `evidenceLevel`. Ban do benh, trang hub, landing bai viet, search index va related content phai sinh tu cung schema; khong duy tri danh sach song song trong page.

### Chuan hoa noi dung y khoa

Scanner tu khoa khong phat hien vi pham truc tiep, nhung review thu cong thay metadata cu co cac cum: `sai lam chet nguoi`, `quyet dinh IQ`, `su that gay soc`, `vo dung`, `dap tat`, `lan ranh sinh tu`.

Can uu tien review cac nhom nguy co cao: thai ky/tre em, than, ung thu, tam than, tu mien, GLP-1, tuong tac thuoc. Tieu de va description phai mo ta loi ich/hanh dong, khong thoi phong quan he nhan qua va khong dung noi so lam dong luc click.

### Quan tri nguon va review

- 310 bai metadata; 293 bai co `sources`, con 17 bai chua khai bao nguon.
- Nhieu bai cu chi link homepage cua to chuc thay vi guideline/trang tai lieu cu the.
- Chua co truong `publishedAt`, `reviewedAt`, `nextReviewAt`, `medicalReviewer`, `evidenceLevel` thong nhat.

Can them quy trinh: reviewer y khoa, ngay review, chu ky review 12-24 thang tuy muc rui ro, link tai lieu cu the, va co che danh dau bai qua han.
